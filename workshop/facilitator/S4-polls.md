## Poll A — "Has validation sent one back?" · launch 0:00

**Why:** the whole session answers one question — *would this pass validation?* — and the
room contains people on both sides of that desk. The poll tells you, in the first two
minutes, whether you are talking to developers who have had a document returned, to people
who have never faced a validator, or to validators. Launch it while `git pull && python
verify.py` runs on their screens.

One question, anonymous, 45 seconds.

**Has a model document of yours been returned by validation?**
- Never
- Once
- More than once
- I'm the validator

**Share the results.** Then read them into the next slide, the 147-line pack against the
SR 11-7 checklist:

- Mostly *more than once*: *"then you know the right-hand column of this table by heart."*
- Mostly *never*: *"this is what the first return usually says."*
- Any *I'm the validator*: name it — *"you're going to be the room's judge at 1:08."* Their
  answers in the H4.2 review carry weight; invite them to go first.
