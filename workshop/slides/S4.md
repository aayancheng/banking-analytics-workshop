# Where the numbers come from

```text
module functions → wiki/facts/facts.json → {{< var score.auc_holdout >}} → page
predict_score_pd      "text": "0.8176"                                     0.8176
                      "population": "held-out 20% of applicants …"
```

- A page never types a number. It cites a **fact**, and the fact names its **population**.
- `python verify.py` recomputes each fact through the modules — or reads it from the committed artifact its source names (`adj.*`); stress refits only under `--full`.
- Change a fact by hand → **`[9/10] Docs: model wiki ... FAIL`**, with the key named.
- Type a number into a page → the lint fails: **hand-typed number**.

# Finding 1 — no out-of-time sample

Every applicant comes from **one** generated cross-section; train and test are a random split.

- Score PSI between the splits: **0.0018**. Features above the **0.10** watch level: **0**.
- That is what a random split *should* show — it measures **sampling noise, not drift**.

**Control:** the monitoring plan — score and feature PSI on each new cohort, with
redevelopment triggers. **Owner:** model developer.

# Finding 2 — the gate population is not the lending population

| Held-out population | n | AUC |
|---|---|---|
| all applicants — **the gate** | 2,400 | **0.8176** |

| rejected only | 756 | 0.8440 |

Gate: **0.78**. Met on all applicants; below it on the booked.

**Control:** report both on every run; track booked-only beside the gate; consider gating on
the booked at redevelopment. The gate itself does not move.

# Finding 3 — features with no causal role are in the model

`industry`, `entity_type`, `trade_lines`: the generator gives them **no** role in default.
All three are among the **15** features.

- Together: **0.4%** of the logit's variance on the held-out applicants.
- Each IV is below the **0.02** "useless" line; a refit on structural zeros ranks at **0.50**.

**Impact:** small — present but inert. They still cost a WoE table each to validate and
monitor, and a reason code with no causal basis. **Control:** remove at redevelopment.

<!-- Speaker: the finding's wording was written after the number existed. We measured first;
     "inert" is what the measurement says, not what we hoped. -->

# Finding 4 — the score is concentrated in one band

**1,222** of **2,400** held-out applicants — **51%** — fall in band D,
where the observed default rate is **28.2%**.

- The band cannot distinguish between the applicants inside it.
- Any use of the band alone — a rate card, a cutoff, a report — treats them as one.

**Control:** downstream decisions use the continuous PD, not the band; pricing by band is a
prohibited use. Review band boundaries at redevelopment.

# Finding 5 — `leverage`'s weight of evidence is not monotonic

In the generator, more leverage **always** means more risk. In the committed card, its WoE
rises and falls across the bins.

- Non-monotonic in bin order: **2** of **13** numeric features — `leverage, trade_lines`.
- `leverage` carries **11.4%** of the logit's variance: this is not a decoy.
- `train.py` leaves `monotonic_trend` at optbinning's `"auto"` default.

**Impact:** not discrimination — defensibility. A leverage reason code in that range cannot
be explained to the applicant. **Control:** set `monotonic_trend` explicitly; re-verify the gate.
