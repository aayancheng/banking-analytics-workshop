7 / 15Finding 1 — no out-of-time sample

Finding 1 — no out-of-time sample

Every applicant comes from one generated cross-section; train and test are a random split.

  • Score PSI between the splits: 0.0018. Features above the 0.10 watch level: 0.
  • That is what a random split should show — it measures sampling noise, not drift.

Control: the monitoring plan — score and feature PSI on each new cohort, with redevelopment triggers. Owner: model developer.

Speaker: Block 0:24–0:27 · D4 finding 1 of 5 Speaker: the stable PSI is not good news; it is no news. Ask: what would an out-of-time sample need that this data does not have? A second cohort.

9 / 15Finding 3 — features with no causal role are in the model

Finding 3 — features with no causal role are in the model

industry, entity_type, trade_lines: the generator gives them no role in default. All three are among the 15 features.

  • Together: 0.4% of the logit's variance on the held-out applicants.
  • Each IV is below the 0.02 "useless" line; a refit on structural zeros ranks at 0.50.

Impact: small — present but inert. They still cost a WoE table each to validate and monitor, and a reason code with no causal basis. Control: remove at redevelopment.

Speaker: Block 0:30–0:33 · D4 finding 3 of 5 Speaker: the finding's wording was written after the number existed. We measured first; "inert" is what the measurement says, not what we hoped.
10 / 15Finding 4 — the score is concentrated in one band

Finding 4 — the score is concentrated in one band

1,222 of 2,400 held-out applicants — 51% — fall in band D, where the observed default rate is 28.2%.

  • The band cannot distinguish between the applicants inside it.
  • Any use of the band alone — a rate card, a cutoff, a report — treats them as one.

Control: downstream decisions use the continuous PD, not the band; pricing by band is a prohibited use. Review band boundaries at redevelopment.

11 / 15Finding 5 — `leverage`'s weight of evidence is not monotonic

Finding 5 — leverage's weight of evidence is not monotonic

In the generator, more leverage always means more risk. In the committed card, its WoE rises and falls across the bins.

  • Non-monotonic in bin order: 2 of 13 numeric features — leverage, trade_lines.
  • leverage carries 11.4% of the logit's variance: this is not a decoy.
  • train.py leaves monotonic_trend at optbinning's "auto" default.

Impact: not discrimination — defensibility. A leverage reason code in that range cannot be explained to the applicant. Control: set monotonic_trend explicitly; re-verify the gate.
