4 / 15A decision is not a score

A decision is not a score

The score estimates risk. The policy layer decides how the bank acts on that estimate.

  1. Hard knockouts — a non-negotiable failure declines the applicant before the model gets the final say.
  2. PD zones — low PD starts in Approve, middle PD starts in Refer, high PD starts in Decline.
  3. Refer overrides — affordability, score, or request-size concerns can downgrade an approval to Refer; they do not upgrade a Decline.
Policy is where risk appetite becomes a customer decision.

6 / 15ROE and RAROC — pricing is where the money happens

ROE and RAROC — pricing is where the money happens

interest income
− cost of funds
− expected loss (PD × LGD)
− operating cost
= pre-tax profit
→ tax → net income
→ net income ÷ allocated equity = ROE

ROE: after-tax net income ÷ allocated equity. Does the loan earn the bank’s required return on capital?

RAROC: pre-tax profit ÷ allocated equity. How attractive is the risk-adjusted economics before tax?

The workshop capital allocation is simplified: allocated equity = 12% × EAD.

7 / 15Quoted rate, hurdle rate, recommended rate

Quoted rate, hurdle rate, recommended rate

break-even rate
  = cost of funds + PD × LGD + operating cost − fees

hurdle-clearing rate
  = break-even rate + after-tax capital return requirement

recommended rate
  = hurdle-clearing rate + base margin
  • Quoted rate: what the booked loan currently carries.
  • Hurdle-clearing rate: the minimum rate needed to reach the 15% ROE hurdle.
  • Recommended rate: the hurdle-clearing rate plus the 2% reference margin cushion.

Business decision: below hurdle → reprice, restructure, or do not book; above hurdle → minimum return clears; above recommended → clears the hurdle plus the cushion.

11 / 15The 54% vs 21.6% trap

The 54% vs 21.6% trap

The whole-book curve can show about 54% capture at 10% reviewed while held-out capture is 21.6%.

  • The whole-book curve includes accounts the model saw during training, so it is optimistic and can rank training-specific noise unusually well.
  • The held-out red point uses unseen accounts and is the honest estimate of future workflow performance.
  • Leakage-safe features prevent direct cheating; held-out evaluation checks generalization.
Quote the held-out number. A model can look brilliant on the book it learned from and still be ordinary on tomorrow’s book.

The gate uses held-out capture ≥ 20% because operational capacity—not an attractive curve— determines whether the watchlist is useful.

12 / 15Precise line-increase offer rules

Precise line-increase offer rules

An offer is a conjunctive gate. Every condition must pass:

candidate probability ≥ 0.3121
AND origination PD ≤ 0.0741
AND recommended amount > $0
AND incremental ROE ≥ 15%

The recommended amount is the smallest of three limits:

min(
  amount needed to reach 65% utilization,
  50% of current credit limit,
  30% of annual revenue − current credit limit
)

The candidate probability asks “is this a good growth candidate?” The origination PD asks “is this account inside risk appetite?” Incremental ROE asks “is the additional exposure worth funding?”
